Improving Supplier Communication in Procurement

Overview

A B2B supply chain web app that helps procurement teams keep supplier communication in one place, so order updates, questions, and changes don't get lost between emails and spreadsheets.

Hypothesis Problem

Purchasing teams struggle to manage supplier communication, follow-ups, and changing order expectations because information is scattered across emails, calls, spreadsheets, and procurement systems.

Findings

57% of chief procurement officers named siloed ways of working as a leading barrier.

Deloitte's 2025 Global Chief Procurement Officer Survey identified siloed ways of working as a leading barrier to procurement teams delivering value. The survey covered more than 250 chief procurement officers across 40 countries.

Source: Deloitte, 2025 Global Chief Procurement Officer Survey

User Research

The biggest challenges in supplier communication

01 Information is scattered

Supplier emails, purchase orders, and delivery updates live across different tools. This makes it harder for purchasers to find accurate, up-to-date information.

Sources: Deloitte (2025); Kembro et al. (2024). Deloitte report ↗ · Academic study ↗

02 Too much time spent chasing updates

Purchasers repeatedly follow up on order confirmations, delivery dates, and unanswered questions. Changing commitments create even more work.

Sources: PO update discussions ↗ · Supplier follow-up discussions ↗

03 Buyers and suppliers aren't always aligned

Both sides may have different information about order status, requirements, or agreed changes. This can lead to confusion, delays, and repeated clarification.

Sources: Deloitte (2025); Kembro et al. (2024). Deloitte report ↗ · Academic study ↗
