Improving Supplier Communication in Procurement

Overview

A B2B supply chain web app that helps procurement teams keep supplier communication in one place, so order updates, questions, and changes don't get lost between emails and spreadsheets.

Hypothesis Problem

Purchasing teams struggle to manage supplier communication, follow-ups, and changing order expectations because information is scattered across emails, calls, spreadsheets, and procurement systems.

Findings

57% of chief procurement officers named siloed ways of working as a leading barrier.

Deloitte's 2025 Global Chief Procurement Officer Survey identified siloed ways of working as a leading barrier to procurement teams delivering value. The survey covered more than 250 chief procurement officers across 40 countries.

Source: Deloitte, 2025 Global Chief Procurement Officer Survey

User Research

What are the pain points, based on existing information?

01

Fragmented information and manual tracking

Purchasers struggle to keep supplier emails, purchase-order records, delivery updates, and follow-ups aligned. Important information can be scattered across tools, making order status harder to verify.

Sources: Deloitte (2025); Kembro et al. (2024)

02

Repeated follow-ups and unclear commitments

Purchasers spend time chasing confirmations, revised delivery dates, and answers to outstanding questions. Changing commitments create additional coordination work.

Sources: purchaser discussions on Reddit about PO updates and follow-up workload. Anecdotal evidence.

03

Inconsistent information sharing

Purchasers and suppliers may not have the same understanding of order status, requirements, or agreed changes. Organizational silos and coordination challenges make information sharing difficult.

Sources: Deloitte (2025); Kembro et al. (2024), a peer-reviewed study involving 29 supply-chain experts.
