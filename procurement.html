Improving Supplier Communication in Procurement

Overview

A B2B supply chain web app that helps procurement teams keep supplier communication in one place, so order updates, questions, and changes don't get lost between emails and spreadsheets.

The Starting Point

My father has over 20 years of experience in supply chain and procurement, helping a major power and water company in the UAE find suppliers, evaluate bids, and manage contracts.

When he began working from home during COVID-19, I got a closer look at his daily work. I noticed how much time he spent coordinating with different teams, following up on decisions, and keeping projects moving.

It got me thinking: could better communication and visibility make this process less time-consuming?

Initial Assumptions

Assumption 01

Fragmented communication

Communication across emails, calls, and different platforms makes it difficult for purchasers and suppliers to track conversations and find the latest updates.

Assumption 02

Limited order visibility

Existing procurement software may not clearly highlight unanswered requests, delayed orders, or changing delivery timelines, leaving purchasers to manually chase updates.

Assumption 03

Delayed supplier decisions

Suppliers and stakeholders may take time to evaluate competing offers, negotiate terms, or assess contract profitability before committing to an order, leaving purchasers uncertain about the outcome and expected timeline.

Primary Research Question

How might we reduce friction in purchaser-supplier collaboration and improve visibility throughout the procurement process?
