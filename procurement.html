Improving Supplier Communication in Procurement

Overview

A B2B supply chain web app that helps procurement teams keep supplier communication in one place, so order updates, questions, and changes don't get lost between emails and spreadsheets.

Hypothesis

Purchasing teams struggle to manage supplier communication, follow-ups, and changing order expectations because information is scattered across emails, calls, spreadsheets, and procurement systems.
