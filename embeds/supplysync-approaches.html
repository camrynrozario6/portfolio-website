Information is scattered

How they bring orders into one place

SAP AribaShared supplier network for purchase orders, confirmations, shipping and invoices
OracleOne portal per purchase order with change history, receipts, invoices and payments
DynamicsVendor collaboration portal linked to the ERP
CoupaSupplier portal showing all of a buyer's purchase orders in one place

Chasing updates

How they cut down follow-ups

SAP AribaFull, partial or split confirmations; automatic reminders for unconfirmed purchase orders
OracleAcknowledgement deadlines; notifications to supplier
DynamicsPurchase order statuses like “In external review” and “Confirmed”
Coupa“Acknowledged” checkbox replaces email confirmations

Misalignment

How they keep both sides in sync

SAP AribaSuppliers confirm revised dates or quantities on the purchase order
OracleSupplier change requests routed to buyer for approval; purchase order revised automatically
DynamicsVendor accepts, rejects or suggests changes; each approved purchase order saved as a version
CoupaComments for change requests; history log on each purchase order

Where it falls short

SAP AribaCan't manually log supplier fulfillment updates
OracleHeavy, enterprise-scale setup
